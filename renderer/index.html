How to use

  1. Accept Battaglia scan missions in-game — they appear here automatically.
  2. Open the contract → screenshot (Win+Shift+S) → OCR from Clipboard to auto-fill requirements.
  3. Or type the counts manually on the mission card and Save.
  4. When you scan an asteroid, pick the resource and hit Record Scan.
  5. Click Overlay (or Ctrl+Shift+O) to show remaining scans over the game.
  6. New mission cards run one automatic OCR — leave the contract DETAILS panel open when accepting.
  7. Progress is shared across every active mission that needs that resource.
